## Claim

Zora pixel A/B had a ~6% noise floor, larger than the changes being measured

## Evidence

Same build, two launches, same camera/entrance: 22640 px (5.9%) differ. The depth-state port measured 3.9% at that camera -- i.e. below its own control. Reduced to 1.18% by the settle primitive.

## What would falsify it

measuring the launch-to-launch control again and finding it materially different, or seeding the effect RNG to push the floor lower

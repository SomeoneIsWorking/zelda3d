## Claim

Three-axis ratio disagreement means CHECK the pairing, not wrong mesh: a modest consistent gap can be a legitimate Grezzo re-authoring

## Evidence

Obj_Syokudai wooden torch: N64 h=58 foot=16x16; syokudai_ki_model (wood, routed) is 20x61x21 giving 0.951/0.800/0.762, while syokudai_isi_model (STONE) is 16x58x16 giving exactly 1.000/1.000/1.000. Matching the numbers would mean rendering the stone mesh for the wooden torch -- chasing an N64 shape instead of porting the OoT3D asset. Contrast a real misidentification: Bg_Ydan_Maruta's wrong candidate was off 200x on Z while the correct one agreed to 1%. So gross mismatch = wrong mesh; modest mismatch consistent across both axes = re-authoring.

## What would falsify it

A pairing with a modest consistent ratio gap is shown to be a genuine misidentification, or a gross mismatch turns out to be a legitimate re-authoring

## Claim

Flat props can now be auto-scaled from a measured FOOTPRINT, and axis agreement is the quality signal

## Evidence

Measure bracket now accumulates world X/Z extents (libultraship-owned globals, so no cross-layer callback signature change). Forest Temple well water resolves at scale=0.97274 with the two axes agreeing to 0.6% (2697.2/2763.4 vs 280.0/288.8). Deku Tree FLOOR web resolves at 0.10090 from n64 240x240 vs model 2378.5x2378.5 with axis spread 0.0%, cross-validating against the WALL web's independently height-derived 0.10000 (within 1%). Flatness is judged RELATIVE (height < 5% of the larger footprint axis) because the floor web measured height 10.0 and an absolute test wrongly used it. zelda_mamenoki derives a scale but with 14% axis spread, which the log flags as a guess.

## What would falsify it

A footprint-derived scale with low axis spread is shown wrong in game, or a prop is found where the N64 footprint legitimately differs in shape from the CMB footprint

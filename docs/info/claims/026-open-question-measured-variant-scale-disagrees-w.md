## Claim

OPEN QUESTION: measured variant scale disagrees with hand-calibrated values by 17-31% and it is NOT known which is right

## Evidence

With selfCalibrate on for all five variants, the two CALIBRATED controls measured rock 0.09998 vs tuned 0.12000 (17% low) and bush 0.65595 vs tuned 0.50000 (31% high), in Hyrule Field. The mechanism is self-consistent (two independent small-rock slots both 0.09998) and dimensionally sound (height/height). So either the hand calibrations were eyeballed wrong, or the measurement double-counts/mis-scopes something (e.g. per-instance actor->scale, or measuring a different instance than was calibrated in Kokiri Forest). NOT resolved -- self-calibration is deliberately scoped to the uncalibrated seeds until it is.

## What would falsify it

Someone measures the same variant in the scene it was originally calibrated in (Kokiri Forest) and either reproduces 0.12 (measurement is scene/instance-dependent) or reproduces 0.09998 (the hand value was wrong)

# Project goals

## G001 — Complete Ocarina of Time 3D presentation on the native PC engine

Run Ocarina of Time through its native PC game-logic engine while replacing the visible and behavioral
seams with grounded Ocarina of Time 3D assets, rendering, animation, cameras, and actor behavior.

## G002 — Complete Majora's Mask 3D presentation on the native PC engine

Apply the same architecture and evidence bar to Majora's Mask, sharing title-neutral asset and renderer
owners while keeping each game's behavior and content policy isolated.

## G003 — Lawful unified player experience

Provide one portable launcher and no-terminal packaged setup that accepts the four player-owned ROMs,
contains no game assets, and stores generated content, settings, and saves in OS user-data locations.
